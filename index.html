Coral Ledger

A private reef journal for one home aquarium.

Coral Ledger is a small app I built for my own reef tank. It keeps a record of each coral I own: where and when I bought it, when it arrived, how I've cared for it, and dated photos of it over time. It also shows my tank's water chemistry alongside that record, so I can see what's growing well and work out what went wrong when a coral doesn't make it.

Who it's for

Just me. Coral Ledger runs on my own computer at home and is only reachable from my own devices. It is not a product, it isn't for sale, there are no other users, and you can't sign up for it.

Why it connects to Google

When I choose to connect my own Google account, Coral Ledger can:

  • Google Photos: let me pick photos of my corals to attach to their records, and save photos I take in the app to my own Google Photos library.

Coral Ledger only connects to Google Photos. Order and shipping receipts reach it separately, by email forward to a dedicated inbox, not through Google sign-in.

It only touches my own account, and it doesn't collect data about anyone else. The details are in the Privacy Policy.
