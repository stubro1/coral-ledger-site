Privacy Policy

Coral Ledger · Last updated October 8, 2026

Coral Ledger is a personal app that I (Stuart Brown) built and run for my own reef aquarium. I am its only user. This page explains, in plain language, what it does with Google account data when I connect my own account.

What data it uses

Coral Ledger only accesses a Google account after I sign in and approve access on Google's own consent screen. It uses that access for:

  • Google Photos: the photos I choose in Google's photo picker, so they can be attached to my coral records, and adding photos I take in the app to my own Google Photos library. It asks for append-only upload access, so it can't read, change, or delete the rest of my library.
  • Gmail (planned, read-only): reading coral order, receipt, and shipping emails so purchase and arrival details can be filled in automatically. It will not send, delete, or change email.

It doesn't collect data about other people and doesn't have any other users.

Where the data is kept

Coral Ledger runs on my own Mac mini at home. The Google sign-in tokens and anything it saves (coral records, photos, order details) are stored on that computer. The app is only reachable from my own devices over my private network. It isn't hosted on a public server.

What it doesn't do

  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of data with anyone.
  • No use of Google data for anything other than my own coral journal.
  • No use of Google data to train AI or machine-learning models.

Coral Ledger's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to remove access

  • Use the Disconnect option in Coral Ledger's Google settings, which deletes the stored tokens from the Mac mini.
  • Or remove Coral Ledger from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data already saved in the journal stays on the Mac mini until I delete it.

Changes

If what Coral Ledger does with Google data changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
